Getting paid in Invoicey is more than stamping a date on an invoice. Every payment — cash, another
bank, or a Fio credit — becomes a **ledger allocation**. Allocations are the source of truth;
invoice status, outstanding totals and the dashboard all derive from them.

```text
  Fio movements  ─┐
                  ├─▶  match proposals  ─▶  you confirm  ─▶  allocation  ─▶  invoice paid / partial / overpaid
  Manual entry   ─┘
```

<Callout title="Confirm-first by design">
  Bank sync only *suggests* matches. Nothing marks an invoice paid until a
  workspace member confirms a proposal or adds a manual payment. Automatic
  confirmation stays off on purpose.
</Callout>

## Before you connect a bank

Matching works best when issued invoices already carry the payment facts Czech banks use:

| Fact                     | Where it comes from                                      |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| **IBAN**                 | Issuer bank account frozen onto the invoice at issue     |
| **Variable symbol (VS)** | Usually the invoice number; used heavily by Fio matching |
| **Amount + currency**    | Invoice total (CZK in the current pilot)                 |

Issue invoices with a clear VS and the correct issuer IBAN before you expect clean proposals. Older
paid invoices already migrated into the ledger as manual allocations — you do not need to re-mark
them.

## Connect Fio banka

The first supported bank is **Fio banka**, via a **read-only monitoring token** from Fio
Internetbanking. That token can pull account movements; it cannot submit payment orders.

Owners and admins configure this under **Settings → Bank connections**. Connections belong to the
**active workspace** — they are never shared with your other workspaces.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Create a monitoring token in Fio

    In Fio Internetbanking, create a token with statement / movement rights only. Copy the
    64-character string once. Invoicey encrypts it at rest and never shows the plaintext again after
    save.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Connect in Invoicey

    Open **Settings → Bank connections**, paste the token, and connect. Invoicey probes Fio,
    verifies the account (CZK pilot), and stores the IBAN on this workspace. One IBAN can be active
    in only one workspace at a time.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Sync movements

    Use **Sync now** on the connection, or wait for the scheduled bank sync. Sync pulls an explicit
    overlapping date range, upserts movements without duplicates, and runs the matcher to open
    proposals on **Payments**.

  </Step>
</Steps>

Disconnect stops further sync but **keeps** imported transactions and allocations — history is not
wiped when you rotate or revoke a token.

## The Payments page

Open **Payments** in the sidebar. It is the reconciliation desk for the workspace.

### Match proposals

Each proposal links a bank credit to an issued invoice, with a score, confidence, and reason /
blocker codes (variable symbol, amount, IBAN, currency, and so on).

| Action      | Effect                                                                      |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Confirm** | Creates an allocation for the proposed amount                               |
| **Reject**  | Dismisses the suggestion; the bank credit stays available for another match |

Confirming a full match marks the invoice paid when allocations cover the absolute total. Confirming
a smaller amount leaves the invoice **partially** paid with an outstanding balance.

### Manual payments

Use **Add a manual payment** for cash, an unconnected bank, or a correction. Pick the outstanding
invoice, amount and paid-on date. Manual entries write the **same ledger** as confirmed Fio matches,
so dashboards stay consistent.

### Recent transactions and allocations

Imported Fio credits show whether they are already allocated. The allocations list is the audit
trail of confirmed payments — reverse an allocation if the wrong invoice was matched; status and
outstanding totals recompute immediately.

## On the invoice itself

Invoice detail shows a **Payment ledger** summary: received, outstanding, and the allocation
timeline. From there you can still **Mark paid** / **Unmark paid** (or reverse a specific
allocation). Those actions are ledger operations too — not a separate shadow field.

When an invoice becomes fully paid, optional **payment-received** email can fire once (configured
per issuer).

## How matching decides

The matcher is deterministic and versioned (`fio-v1` today). It never crosses workspace boundaries
and never invents FX: transaction currency, allocation currency and invoice currency must agree.

Typical strong signals:

- Variable symbol matches the issued invoice
- Amount equals (or cleanly covers) the outstanding balance
- Credit lands on the invoice’s payment IBAN

Ambiguous or blocked cases stay as proposals (or stay unmatched) until a human decides. That is
preferable to silently marking the wrong invoice paid.

## Partial, split and overpaid

| Situation                            | What Invoicey does                                                   |
| ------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Client pays part of the total        | Allocation recorded; invoice stays open with outstanding             |
| One transfer covers several invoices | Confirm each proposal (or add manual splits) as separate allocations |
| Client overpays                      | Invoice can show **overpaid**; issued PDF/ISDOC totals stay frozen   |
| You matched the wrong invoice        | **Reverse** the allocation; rematch from Payments                    |

Issued documents are never rewritten when money moves — only payment projections change.

## What this does not include yet

- Payment initiation or any write-capable bank token
- Automatic confirmation without a human
- Foreign-exchange / cross-currency allocation
- Banks other than Fio (the ledger is ready for more adapters later)
- Full rematch / multi-invoice split UI polish beyond confirm, reject, reverse and manual add

## Tips

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="Proposals never appear after sync">
    Confirm the connection status is active, the sync finished without an error
    badge, and you have issued invoices whose VS / IBAN / CZK amount can match
    the credit. Drafts are never candidates.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I use another bank">
    Keep using **Mark paid** or **Add a manual payment**. Fio is the first feed;
    other banks are not connected yet, but the ledger already accepts manual
    entries the same way.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Token or sync errors">
    Use a monitoring-only token for a CZK account. Fio rate-limits roughly every
    30 seconds per token — wait briefly between **Sync now** attempts. Rotate
    the token from Bank connections if Fio revoked the old one.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>
