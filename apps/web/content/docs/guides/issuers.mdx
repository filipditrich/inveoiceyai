title: Issuers

An **issuer** is a business that issues invoices. A workspace can hold several — your OSVČ trade and
your s.r.o., or two companies you run — each with its own numbering, bank account and branding.

## Creating one

**Issuers → New.** Enter the IČO and Invoicey fills the legal name, address and DIČ from
[ARES](/docs/guides/clients#ares-lookup).

ARES cannot tell you everything, so these are yours to supply:

<Steps>

<Step>

### Bank account

Two fields, both required:

| Field          | Format                     | Example                    |
| -------------- | -------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Account number | `[prefix-]number/bankCode` | `19-2000145399/0800`       |
| IBAN           | `CZ` + 22 digits           | `CZ6508000000192000145399` |
| BIC            | Optional, 8 or 11 chars    | `GIBACZPX`                 |

Both are validated. The account number is what prints on the PDF; the IBAN is what goes into the
[payment QR code](/docs/outputs/payment-qr). They must describe the same account — nothing checks
this for you, and a mismatch means clients scanning the QR pay a different account than the one
printed above it.

</Step>

<Step>

### VAT payer flag

Whether you charge DPH. Turning it **off** forces every line on every invoice to 0 % and replaces the
VAT recap on the PDF with _Nejsem plátce DPH_. See [Czech VAT](/docs/concepts/czech-vat).

</Step>

<Step>

### Contact email

Required. It becomes the `Reply-To` on every invoice email sent for this issuer, so client replies
reach you rather than the sending domain.

</Step>

<Step>

### Registry note

The _zápis v obchodním rejstříku_ line Czech invoices carry:

> Společnost zapsaná v obchodním rejstříku vedeném Krajským soudem v Brně, oddíl C, vložka 12345.

Sole traders generally use:

> Fyzická osoba zapsaná v živnostenském rejstříku.

Optional, but if your company has one, it belongs on your invoices.

</Step>

<Step>

### Numbering scheme

Per document type. The default `{YYYY}{####}` gives `20260001`. Full token list and reset behaviour
in [numbering](/docs/concepts/numbering).

</Step>

</Steps>

## Logos, stamps and signatures

Three optional images placed on the PDF:

| Asset           | Formats        | Max size |
| --------------- | -------------- | -------- |
| Logo            | PNG, JPEG, SVG | 1 MB     |
| Stamp (razítko) | PNG, JPEG      | 1 MB     |
| Signature       | PNG, JPEG      | 1 MB     |

Uploads go through UploadThing and need `UPLOADTHING_TOKEN` configured. Without it the fields still
accept pasted URLs, so a self-hosted instance without an UploadThing account is not blocked — it just
has to host the images somewhere itself.

Replacing an asset uploads a new file and leaves the old URL alive. That is on purpose: invoices you
issued last year hold the _old_ URL in their [snapshot](/docs/concepts/snapshots) and must keep
rendering with the logo you had at the time.

## Email settings

Per issuer, used at send time:

| Setting                 | Default                           |
| ----------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Default subject         | `Faktura {number} — {issuerName}` |
| Default cover text      | —                                 |
| Attach ISDOC by default | on                                |
| Display name template   | `{issuerName} via Invoicey`       |
| Overdue reminders       | off                               |
| Reminder interval       | 7 days                            |
| Payment-received email  | off                               |

These are read **live** when an email is sent, not frozen into snapshots — so fixing a bad default
subject immediately improves future sends of invoices you issued months ago.

## Editing an issuer

Edits apply to **future** invoices only. Already-issued invoices hold frozen copies and will not
change — see [snapshots](/docs/concepts/snapshots).

This means rebranding, moving office or switching banks is a safe, ordinary edit. It also means
correcting a mistake does not retroactively fix invoices that already went out with it.

## Presets for automation

Issuers can be saved as **presets** for the [MCP](/docs/integrations/mcp) and
[Slack](/docs/integrations/slack) tools. A preset locks the "from" party so an assistant creating an
invoice cannot invent an IČO or a bank account — it selects a preset by ID and the issuer block is
injected server-side.

Given that a hallucinated bank account on an invoice means a client wiring money to nobody, this is
the part of the automation design worth understanding before you use it.
