## Issuing

**Issue** is the one irreversible action in Invoicey. It happens in a single database transaction:

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Validate The draft payload is parsed against the full schema. Anything
    missing or malformed stops here.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Allocate a number The issuer's [numbering
    scheme](/docs/concepts/numbering) row is locked, the next number computed,
    and the counter incremented — with a yearly reset if the issue date crossed
    into a new year.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Freeze snapshots The live issuer and client are copied onto the invoice.
    From now on it is [immune to edits](/docs/concepts/snapshots) of those
    records.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Recompute totals Totals are recalculated server-side and compared
    against what was submitted. A mismatch aborts.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Render and store The PDF and ISDOC are generated and uploaded, and their
    URLs stored on the invoice so they are served rather than re-rendered on
    every download.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If any step fails, everything rolls back — **including the counter**. A failed issue does not burn a
number.

<Callout type="warn" title="There is no edit after this">
  Issued invoices are frozen. If something is wrong, cancel and re-issue, or
  issue a credit note. See
  [why](/docs/concepts/statuses#why-issued-invoices-are-frozen).
</Callout>

## Recording payment

**Mark paid** stamps the payment date; the [status](/docs/concepts/statuses) derives to `paid` and
the invoice leaves your overdue list.

You can supply the actual date the money arrived rather than accepting today — worth doing, since the
overdue reminder cron and your dashboard both read it.

**Unmark paid** clears it if you mark the wrong invoice. The status re-derives from the remaining
facts, which may land on `overdue` if the due date has since passed.

<Callout>
  Nothing marks invoices paid automatically. There is no bank connection and no
  payment matching — you tell Invoicey when money arrives.
</Callout>

## Overdue

An invoice goes overdue at the **end of its due date, Europe/Prague time** — not midnight UTC.

Optional automated reminders exist per issuer, off by default, sending every 7 days by default to the
client's contact email. Turn them on in the issuer's email settings. They require the deployment's
reminder cron to be scheduled — see [sending email](/docs/guides/sending-email#deployment-setup).

## Cancelling

Cancelling stamps a cancellation date. The invoice stays in the system, keeps its number forever, and
displays as `stornováno`.

The number is deliberately not released. A gap in your invoice sequence is a question you have to
answer during a tax inspection; a cancelled invoice sitting in the sequence answers it.

## Downloading

PDF and ISDOC are available from the invoice detail. Both are served from stored copies made at issue
time rather than re-rendered, so the file a client received and the file you download later are
byte-identical.

Imported historical invoices are marked immutable and always serve the **original uploaded PDF** —
Invoicey never regenerates over a document another system produced.
